I am currently a Master's student at [Stanford University](https://www.stanford.edu/), where I've been fortunate enough to work on robot learning with [Jeannette Bohg](https://web.stanford.edu/~bohg/) and [Dorsa Sadigh](https://dorsa.fyi/). I have also had the opportunity to collaborate with [Tom Silver](https://tomsilver.github.io/) at [Princeton PRPL](https://prpl-group.com/).

My research is interested in answering (1) **how can we achieve large-scale robot data collection with limited human supervision**; (2) **what makes some robotics data more useful than others, and how can we exploit these useful properties for better downstream policy learning**? Before Stanford, I studied computer science at [Georgia Tech](https://www.gatech.edu/), where I specialized in artificial intelligence and low-level systems.

*I am applying to PhD programs in Robotics for Fall 2027.*
