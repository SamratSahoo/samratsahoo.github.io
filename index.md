I am currently a Master's student at [Stanford University](https://www.stanford.edu/), where I've been fortunate enough to work on robot learning with [Jeannette Bohg](https://web.stanford.edu/~bohg/) and [Dorsa Sadigh](https://dorsa.fyi/). I have also had the opportunity to collaborate with [Tom Silver](https://tomsilver.github.io/) at [Princeton PRPL](https://prpl-group.com/).

My research is interested in answering (1) **how can we achieve large-scale robot data collection with limited human supervision** and (2) **how can we use our collected data to train policies that generalize reliably to real-world execution**? Before Stanford, I studied computer science at Georgia Tech, where I specialized in artificial intelligence and low-level systems.
